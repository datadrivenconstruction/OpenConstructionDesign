# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas:** open a thread in
  [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/datadrivenconstruction/OpenConstructionDesign/discussions).
- **Bugs:** open an [issue](https://github.com/datadrivenconstruction/OpenConstructionDesign/issues/new/choose) with the version, the
  steps to reproduce and, if you can share it, a small model that shows the problem.
- **Security problems:** do not open a public issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Build and usage help:** [docs/BUILDING.md](docs/BUILDING.md) and
  [docs/USER_GUIDE.md](docs/USER_GUIDE.md).

This is a community project; maintainers answer as time allows.
