# What's new

## 2026.11
- Built-in help: `F1` opens the topic for the active tool, the selected element or the view; topic search and clickable command links.
- Interactive "First steps" tutorial and a sample two-storey house: see a finished model right away.
- Forms: a tab of 24 commands - extrude, revolve, sweep, blend, void forms, booleans, fillet, chamfer, shell.
- Push/Pull in 3D: face highlight, drag with a live preview and typing a distance.
- Walls, floors, roofs and curtain walls on form faces with stable face labels: elements follow the form when it changes.
- Wall editing: joint variants (butt, miter), layer priorities, compound walls, profiles, grooves and attaching the top to a roof or slab.
- Curtain wall editing: grid segments, pinned mullions, panel types, corner joints, slope and arc.
- Roofs by extrusion, dormers and roof openings, plus shafts that cut every floor, roof and ceiling on their way.
- IFC import: any product with geometry becomes a native element or an editable mesh; re-import updates elements by GlobalId.
- Query in the `Ctrl+K` palette: `doors width>900 -> width=width+100` changes the result in one undoable operation.
- Toposolid: from points and from a survey file, boundary, sub-regions, building pad with cut and fill, smoothing, contour lines.
- Structure: column, strip and slab footings, braces, trusses, beam systems.
- Roofs: gable, hip, mansard and other shapes, roof joins, fascias, gutters, wall sweeps and cornices.
- 3D view: visual styles, walk and fly, saved 3D views, section box from selection, temporary category hiding.
- Large files open in the background; the mesh cache speeds up 3D for imported meshes.

## 2026.10
- `.xlsx` exchange: export and import of all categories with a diff preview, units in headers and one `Ctrl+Z` to revert.
- Sheets and printing: A0-A4 and ANSI sizes, viewports, sheet sets, sheets per level, output to PDF and SVG.
- Sections with an exact cut and lines beyond the plane, elevations per compass side, a section box in 3D.
- Sun shadows, soft corner shading, silhouettes and correct glass sorting in 3D.
- Structure: footings, beam systems, braces, trusses, reinforcement with a bending schedule, the analytical model.
- MEP: ducts, pipes, trays, equipment, systems, auto-route and connectivity check.
- Clash detection with a report; rule-based checks and a 3D version compare.
- DWG, DXF and PDF import as underlays; our own DWG reader.
- Dimensions: linear, angular, radial, automatic wall chain, temporary dimensions on the selection.
- Five ready-made interfaces (`Ctrl+Alt+1...5`), autosave and crash recovery.
