## Values taken from standards and building codes

<!-- PENDING LEGAL APPROVAL: numbers-only disclaimer below; remove this line once approved. -->

Steel section dimensions, bolt and weld sizes, paper sizes, line weights and the numeric limits in
the rule packs are factual values taken from published standards and building regulations. The
project compiled them into its own tables and wrote its own one-line descriptions; no text, tables
or figures of any standard are reproduced. Each rule names its source document and clause so that
users can check the current edition.

These values are provided for convenience and are not a substitute for the official documents.
Codes and standards change and are adopted differently by each jurisdiction. Always verify against
the edition that applies to your project. Titles of standards are used only to identify them;
standards bodies do not endorse this project.

The UK rule pack (`crates/model/data/norms/packs/uk/`) contains public sector information
licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0

## Sample models (CC0-1.0)

`crates/model/data/gltf/` contains BoxVertexColors, MetalRoughSpheresNoTextures, SimpleMeshes and
Triangle from the Khronos glTF Sample Assets (https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets,

`manifest.tsv`; the CC0 text is in `licenses/CC0-1.0.txt`.

## Not included

Real project models and drawings used for testing are not part of this repository. Tests that need
them are disabled by default and read the files from paths given in environment variables.
